# Design Tokens

## Core colors

- Dark: background `#0E0E0F`, surface `#171719`, border `#2D2D31`, text `#F7F7F8`, accent `#C9A24A`.
- Light: background `#F7F7F8`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#E3E5E8`, text `#18181B`, accent `#717B88`.
- Semantic: success `#22C55E`, warning `#F59E0B`, danger `#EF4444`, info `#3B82F6`.

## Scale

- Spacing: `4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48`.
- Radius: input/button `10`, card `16`, modal `20`, pill `999`.
- Font: Inter hoặc Geist; body `14/20`, small `12/16`, H1 `24/32`.
